About PrimeTurf

Premium artificial turf, installed by our own teams

PrimeTurf delivers precision-installed artificial turf across Gauteng and the Western Cape's most sought-after neighbourhoods and estates — from individual garden replacements to large-scale estate and commercial installations.

How we work

One standard, whatever the size of the job

Every project receives the same premium materials, professional preparation and precision finish — engineered for the area's specific conditions. A 30m² courtyard is scoped the same way a 300m² estate lawn is.

We do not subcontract. Every installation is supplied and installed by our own trained teams under full supervision, which is the only way to guarantee workmanship for 24 months and mean it.

Why PrimeTurf

Built for South African conditions

Zero water

Eliminate lawn irrigation permanently. No restrictions risk, no water bills.

Climate ready

UV-stabilised turf engineered for Gauteng's high-altitude sun and Cape Town's salt-air coastal exposure.

All-weather drainage

Engineered drainage handles Gauteng's summer downpours and Cape Town's winter rainfall without pooling or run-off.

Invisible seams

Consistent pile direction across every square metre, with an engineered sub-base built for clay and coastal soils.
